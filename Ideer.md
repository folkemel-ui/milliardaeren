# Ideer

The idea list for a business-empire game inspired by *Business Empire: RichMan*. Nothing is set in stone. We build in packs of 3–4 items at a time. When an item is done it is removed from this list and the list is renumbered; the git log holds the history. Refer to items by title, not number.

Stack: React + Vite + TypeScript, pure seeded engine (no `Math.random`/`Date.now` in the engine), Norwegian UI, NOK, no new dependencies.

## Pack plan

A suggested order, grouped so each pack feels complete when played. The order is a suggestion; packs can be swapped or reshuffled at any time. Items are referred to by title.

18. **Farm machines priced on the harvest.** Machines and buildings (all but the greenhouse) cost a share of land + everything built (`gardsverdi`), and ten fields are 16.1× land: the tractor pays back in 14.9 h on a 3-field farm and 75.8 h on a 10-field one (frost cover 12.8 → 65 h, drainage 15 → 75 h, combine 20 → 101 h, cowshed with cows 19.5 → 33.4 h), so growing the fields first costs 5× for the same %. Price them on land × fields ÷ 3, so payback is the same at any size, and show «≈ kr X i uka · tjent inn på N t» on each machine, building and field row.
19. **A silo that sells, and a barn with a job.** With a silo the grain fills four weeks of storage and loses 1 % a week until you sell by hand: −3.8 % of the grain a year by default. A third setting «Lagre billig, selg dyrt» that stores when last week’s grain price is under ×1.07 and sells at ×1.02 or more (+14 % on grain over 312 weeks of the real price hash). The barn adds +2.4 points for 30 % of the farm’s value (~115 h): let it store the vegetables and the greenhouse’s crop as the silo stores grain, or cut its price to ~5 %.
20. **A sprayer worth having.** A bad week falls 30 % less: 48.5 h payback alone, ~700 h after drainage and frost cover (the weakest week 0.53 → 0.60). Add +8 % harvest: 17 h alone, 32 h after both.
21. **Fields that pay back.** Each field costs ×1.5 the last for the same harvest: fields 4–10 pay back in 10.5, 16, 24, 36, 54, 80 and 121 h, so 8–10 never do in a ~34 h game. ×1.25: 11, 13, 17, 21, 26, 32 and 40 h, all seven 7.5× land instead of 16.1×.
22. **Animals that differ, and wool you can see.** Every animal returns 4 % of its price a week (`dyr.ts`), so the only difference is price per slot and cows win every one (hens 42.5–112 h against cows 19.5–33 h). Give each its own return (e.g. cow 3.5 %, pig 5 %, sheep 5.5 %, hen 6 % a week — to be measured). Sheep are shorn on 1 May and 1 September, and the wool waiting (up to ~2.2× land for twenty sheep) is neither shown nor in net worth: count it and show «Ull så langt kr X · klippes 1. mai».
23. **Forests that say when to fell.** The card shows only «Tømmer kr X etter N dager»; growth peaks at day 18 and the best rotation is day 36–48 (25–28 % of land an hour, more than any property), while day 108 gives 18.8 % and day 216 12.2 % — and the help text says growth is fastest the first 36 days. Show «Vokser nå kr X/dag» and «Best å hogge om N dager» with a «Klar for hogst» chip, and fix the text.
24. **Farmland that follows its region.** Land rises a fixed 0.3 % a day (×3.4 over the bench game against ~×1.35 for the property index, never a fall), though Hedmarken, Lista, Namdalen and Trysil sit in `REGIONER`. Multiply by the region’s factor (about ±6 %), optionally the cycle, keeping 0.3 %/day as the base.
